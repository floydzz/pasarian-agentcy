# About Embun

Embun ("dew" in Bahasa Melayu) is a Malaysian skincare brand founded in 2021 in
Petaling Jaya. We formulate for skin that lives in 32°C heat and 80% humidity all
year round — not for the dry-winter routines most global brands are built around.
Everything is made in Malaysia, JAKIM halal-certified, and NPRA-notified.

# Positioning

We sit between pharmacy basics and imported K-beauty: RM39–RM89, dermatologist-
reviewed, sold through our own Shopee and Lazada stores plus 40 Watsons doors.
The competitive claim we own is *humidity-first formulation*. Nobody else in this
price band talks about sebum-humidity interaction; we always do.

# Brand voice

Warm, plainspoken, a little funny. We write the way a knowledgeable older sister
explains things — never clinical, never breathless.

Bilingual by default: English base with natural Manglish and Bahasa Melayu code-
switching ("lepak", "jom", "kulit berminyak", "memang okay"). Code-switch once or
twice per caption at most; forced slang reads as a brand pretending to be young.

Sentence rhythm is short. We ask questions. We use "you", never "consumers".

# Voice guardrails — never do these

- **Never promise whitening, fairness, or "putih".** This is a permanent brand
  line, not a legal minimum. Say "even tone" or "brighter" only when referring to
  post-acne marks, never to baseline skin colour.
- **No medical claims.** We cannot say "cures acne", "treats eczema", or
  "dermatologist-approved" (we say "dermatologist-reviewed"). NPRA rules on
  cosmetic notification prohibit therapeutic claims.
- **No before/after photos** of a named condition, and no retouched skin texture.
- **No fear-based copy.** We do not tell people their pores are shameful.
- **No overclaiming timelines.** "In 4 weeks, 8 in 10 testers said..." only when
  we have the panel data; otherwise no number at all.
- **Never imply halal certification covers efficacy** — it covers ingredients and
  process only.

# Tone by channel

- **TikTok** — first person, hook in the first 1.5 seconds, one idea per video.
  Humour allowed. Founder or staff on camera beats polished studio footage.
- **Instagram** — softer, editorial. Captions can run to three lines.
- **Shopee / Lazada** — functional and scannable. Lead with the problem the
  product solves and the bundle price. No poetry.
- **WhatsApp broadcast** — short, one CTA, always with an opt-out line.

# Visual identity

Palette: dew green (#2F6B57), soft sand (#EDE3D3), off-black text (#1B1B18).
Photography is bright, natural daylight, visible skin texture, real Malaysian
faces across Malay, Chinese, and Indian representation — this is non-negotiable
in any cast of more than one person. Product always sits on a matte surface, no
glossy studio reflections. Type is set in Söhne or a close humanist sans.
