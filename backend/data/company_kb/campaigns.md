# What has worked

**"Lepak tapi glowing" (Raya 2026, TikTok + IG).** Founder-led, shot on a phone
in a Klang Valley mamak at 11pm. Six variants around one hook: skincare that
survives a night out in the heat. Best CPM we have ever recorded; the winning
variant was the one where the founder admitted her own skin was breaking out.
Lesson: candour outperforms polish for us, consistently.

**No-white-cast sunscreen demo (April 2026).** Split-face swatch on four skin
tones, no voiceover, text on screen only. Travelled far beyond our follower base.
Lesson: a visible, single-take demo beats explanation.

**Shopee 6.6 bundle push.** Creative that led with the bundle price outperformed
creative that led with the product benefit — on marketplace surfaces only. On
TikTok the same price-led creative underperformed badly.

# What has not worked

**"Glass skin" trend-chasing (Jan 2026).** We ran a glass-skin campaign because
the hashtag was surging. It converted poorly and drew comments accusing us of
copying Korean brands. Lesson: a trend must be translated into our humidity-first
angle or skipped entirely. Borrowing the aesthetic wholesale reads as inauthentic.

**Influencer-heavy launch for the barrier cream (Nov 2025).** Twelve mid-tier
creators, near-identical scripts, no distinct angles. Diminishing returns after
the third post. Lesson: variation across creative *axes* matters more than volume.

**Long-form educational carousels on TikTok.** Died. That content belongs on IG.

# Standing calendar

Ramadan and Raya (largest window of the year, plan 6 weeks ahead), Merdeka in
August, the 9.9 / 10.10 / 11.11 / 12.12 platform mega-sales, Chinese New Year,
Deepavali, and the November–February monsoon when humidity messaging peaks.

# Audience

Core: Malaysian women 22–34, urban Klang Valley, Penang, Johor Bahru, mixed
Malay / Chinese / Indian, phone-first, discovering on TikTok and buying on
Shopee. Price-sensitive but brand-loyal once converted. Secondary: men 25–35
buying the cleanser and sunscreen only, usually on Lazada.
