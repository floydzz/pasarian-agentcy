# Embun hydrating serum (hero)

RM69 / 30ml. Our best seller — roughly 45% of revenue. A lightweight
niacinamide 4% + polyglutamic acid serum aimed at humidity-clogged pores: skin
that is oily on the surface and dehydrated underneath. Absorbs in under 30
seconds, which is the single detail that converts in video. Fragrance-free.
Suitable under sunscreen and under makeup.

Claim we can make: "8 in 10 of our 120-person Malaysian test panel said skin felt
less greasy by week 4." Claims we cannot make: anything about acne treatment.

# Embun gel cleanser

RM39 / 150ml. Amino-acid surfactant, pH 5.5, no SLS. Positioned as the
"double-cleanse without the tightness" step. Best-performing angle historically
is the tudung-and-humidity angle: sweat trapped along the hairline.

# Embun barrier cream

RM79 / 50ml. Ceramide + squalane, deliberately light for the climate. Slowest
seller. Our own diagnosis is that "cream" reads as heavy to Malaysian buyers;
the product does better when we lead with "gel-cream" and show it disappearing
into skin rather than sitting on it.

# Embun daily sunscreen SPF50+ PA++++

RM89 / 50ml. Launched March 2026. Chemical filter, no white cast — the no-white-
cast demo on deeper skin tones is the highest-performing creative we have ever
run. Water-resistant 40 minutes. Reapplication messaging is a standing education
theme for us.
