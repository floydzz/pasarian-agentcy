# How to read this file

Hand-curated Malaysian social and marketplace signals, compiled 19 August 2026
from TikTok Creative Center (MY, last 30 days), Shopee MY category pages, and
Lazada MY trending searches. These platforms have no usable public API, so this
file is maintained manually and dated. Treat every entry as **inspiration, not
fact** — a trend justifies an angle, it never justifies a product claim.

# TikTok MY — hashtag and format signals

**#SkinbarrierMY is climbing.** Barrier-repair language has moved from
dermatology creators into mainstream beauty content. Framing has shifted from
"fix your damaged skin" to "stop over-exfoliating" — a permission-giving,
do-less message rather than a corrective one.

**Sunscreen reapplication content is unusually strong.** Powder and stick
reapplication demos over makeup are the dominant format. Comment sections are
full of people asking what to do while wearing a tudung or a full face.

**"Get ready with me, Malaysian humidity edition."** A recurring native format:
creators film a routine, then cut to themselves visibly sweating two hours later.
The honesty of the second shot is what earns the saves.

**Text-on-screen product demos, no voiceover.** Still outperforming talking-head
explainers in the MY beauty category, and travels across language groups because
it needs no translation.

**Merdeka creative is already ramping.** Local-pride framing peaks 25–31 August.
Buying Malaysian-made is the through-line; flag-waving without substance is being
called out in comments this year.

# Shopee MY — commerce signals

- **9.9 pre-sale vouchers are already live.** Buyers are adding to cart in late
  August and holding until 9 September; creative that names the sale date is
  outperforming urgency copy that does not.
- **Bundles beat single SKUs** in the skincare category. Two-step routines
  (cleanser + serum) convert better than four-step sets at this price point.
- **Trending searches**: "serum untuk kulit berminyak", "sunscreen no white
  cast", "skincare halal", "toner viral".
- **Live commerce** in beauty is heavily discount-led; brand-story lives are
  underperforming against straight demo-and-price lives.

# Lazada MY — commerce signals

- Male grooming is growing faster than the category average; cleanser and
  sunscreen dominate the male basket.
- **LazMall trust signals** — authenticity badges and free-return labels — are
  being surfaced in creative by competitors and appear to lift conversion.
- Refill and value-size formats are trending as an inflation-response angle.

# Cross-platform themes worth borrowing

1. **Do less, not more** — routine simplification as the antidote to 10-step
   fatigue.
2. **Climate honesty** — content that admits Malaysian heat defeats most routines
   earns trust.
3. **Local-made pride** — strongest through end-August, and it must be backed by
   something real (where it is made, who makes it).
4. **Reapplication education** — an under-served, high-intent question.

# Explicitly cooling off

Glass-skin content is past its peak in MY and now reads as derivative. The
10-step routine framing is being actively mocked. Avoid both unless the campaign
subverts them deliberately.
